# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background, identity or experience. Keep issues,
pull requests and reviews respectful and focused on the work.

- Critique ideas and code without insulting people.
- Be patient with questions and differing levels of experience.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information or private communications.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexual content or spam.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations, close disruptive
discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and
consistent across contributors.

For a public discussion, you can ask a maintainer to intervene without repeating harmful material.
For sensitive incidents or abuse, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls.
Do not post private details in an issue to report misconduct.

These expectations apply to this project's GitHub spaces and when representing the project.
